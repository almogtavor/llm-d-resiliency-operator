Failures

Every failure IRO handles, one page each. Each page shows the failure step by step: the group's Pods and members, the controller's phase, and every call IRO makes with its input and output.

How IRO recovers​

  1. Remove in place. When the engine proves members are gone, one reconfig removes them. The rest keep serving.
  2. Restore capacity. Once the group serves, IRO resets the failed Pods once and admits their new members.
  3. Reset as the last resort. When nothing can be removed safely, or a recovery itself fails, IRO resets the whole group. It never gives up on its own: the workload's restart policy paces resets.
  4. Never act blind. IRO never removes a member it cannot see, and never resets a group it cannot list.

A fault always comes first: capacity work waits until the group serves again.
